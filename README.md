# 🎸 FretFlow — Modal Mastery Engine for Guitarists

> **FretFlow** is a next-generation practice companion that turns the fretboard into a living map of musical modes. Inspired by the original *Guitar-Mode-Trainer* concept, FretFlow expands the idea into a full modal ecosystem — a place where the seven modes of the major scale, spread across all seven diatonic key centers, become second nature on both the E and A string roots.

Whether you are a bedroom shredder chasing the Dorian shimmer, a jazz student decoding Locrian tension, or a session player who needs to pivot between Lydian brightness and Phrygian darkness on a moment's notice, FretFlow gives you a structured, repeatable, and surprisingly fun path toward total modal fluency.

## 🧭 Overview

FretFlow is built on a simple but powerful premise: **modes are not trivia — they are geography**. Every mode is a landscape with its own emotional weather, and the fretboard is the terrain you traverse. Most guitarists learn modes as shapes and stop there. FretFlow pushes further, drilling the *sound* of each mode in every key, anchored to the two most common root strings on the instrument.

The engine presents you with randomized prompts such as "A Aeolian, root on the A string" or "F# Dorian, root on the E string," and you respond by playing the corresponding pattern. Over time, your fingers and ears begin to anticipate the modal color before your conscious mind catches up. That anticipatory fluency is the real goal — the moment a mode stops being a formula and starts being a mood you can summon on demand.

The project name, *FretFlow*, reflects the end state: a state of effortless movement across the neck where modal choices feel like instinct rather than calculation.

## 🎯 Why Modal Training Matters

Modal knowledge unlocks improvisational freedom. When you understand that a single parent scale can generate seven distinct emotional palettes, you stop hunting for "the right note" and start *choosing* the atmosphere you want to create. This applies across genres:

- **Rock and metal** — Dorian and Phrygian riffs dominate the darker side of the spectrum.
- **Jazz and fusion** — Lydian and Mixolydian colors are staples of sophisticated soloing.
- **Folk and acoustic** — Aeolian and Ionian anchor countless beloved progressions.
- **Progressive and experimental** — Locrian's instability fuels tension and release.

FretFlow treats each of these as a first-class citizen, ensuring you can move between them without hesitation.

## 🎵 The Seven Modes Explained

Each mode carries a unique interval recipe relative to its parent major scale. FretFlow reinforces these recipes through repetition and contextual prompts.

| Mode | Flavor | Characteristic Tone |
|------|--------|---------------------|
| Ionian | Bright and resolved | Major 7th |
| Dorian | Smooth with a bittersweet edge | Major 6th over minor 3rd |
| Phrygian | Dark and Spanish-tinged | Minor 2nd |
| Lydian | Dreamy and floating | Augmented 4th |
| Mixolydian | Warm and bluesy | Minor 7th |
| Aeolian | Melancholic and natural | Minor 6th |
| Locrian | Unstable and tense | Diminished 5th |

FretFlow rotates through all seven so no mode gets neglected, and you can weight the rotation toward whichever mode currently gives you trouble.

## 🎯 Key Centers on the E and A Strings

The E and A strings are the gravitational anchors of most guitar vocabulary. Nearly every scale shape, chord voicing, and riff pattern has a natural home rooted on one of them. FretFlow exploits this by centering every drill on those two anchors, which means the skills you build translate directly into real playing situations.

For each key center, FretFlow presents:

1. The prompt (mode + key + root string).
2. A response window where you play the pattern.
3. Feedback on accuracy and speed.
4. A rolling average that tracks improvement across sessions.

## 🛠️ Practice Workflow

A typical FretFlow session unfolds like this:

1. **Warm-up** — A short rotation covering all seven modes at a slow tempo.
2. **Focused drill** — Targeted practice on one or two modes that need work.
3. **Speed round** — Timed prompts to build reflex-level recall.
4. **Aural pass** — Listen to each mode's characteristic color without playing.
5. **Cool-down** — A relaxed rotation to consolidate the session.

You can customize the balance between these phases, and FretFlow remembers your preferences between visits.
